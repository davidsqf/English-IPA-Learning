# Source register

Checked on 2026-10-18. IDs are stable references used in seed data. “Opened” means web text was retrieved, not that every interactive control or audio clip was tested. Do not treat a source's public availability as permission to redistribute it.

## S01 — Official IPA chart

[International Phonetic Association: The IPA Chart](https://www.internationalphoneticassociation.org/node/1061). Opened. Authority for the international chart, its revision history, and chart reproduction terms. The chart in IPA Kiel and subparts are offered under CC BY-SA 4.0; that statement does not license every recording on the website.

## S02 — Official interactive IPA chart

[Interactive IPA Chart](https://www.internationalphoneticassociation.org/IPAcharts/IPA_charts_TI/IPA_charts_TI.html). Opened, with incomplete dynamic rendering. Supports symbol descriptions, Unicode information, transcription entry, multilingual labels, and recordings credited to Esling, House, Ladefoged, and Wells. General IPA reference, not an English accent course.

## S03 — Interactive chart permissions

[About IPA i-charts](https://www.internationalphoneticassociation.org/IPAcharts/common_files/IPA_charts_about.html). Opened, but dynamic audio-permission and licence sections were incomplete in retrieved text. Indexed text advertises CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 for the interactive chart. Individual recording permissions remain unresolved here; link out rather than package its audio.

## S04 — IPA Association resource directory

[Links to Phonetics Resources](https://www.internationalphoneticassociation.org/content/links-phonetics-resources). Search-indexed official directory. Supports the selection of established phonetics resources, including ipachart.com and university teaching tools. Not an endorsement of every site's current accuracy or uptime.

## S05 — British Council teacher chart

[Phonemic chart for English teachers](https://www.teachingenglish.org.uk/teaching-resources/teaching-secondary/teaching-tools/phonemic-chart-english-teachers). Opened. Supports chart-based pronunciation teaching and contrast activities. The page currently lists six diphthongs in one passage while also referring to 44 symbol posters: do not use that passage as the definitive inventory count.

## S06 — British Council Sounds Right

[LearnEnglish Sounds Right](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/apps/learnenglish-sounds-right). Opened. Describes the mobile app's tap-to-hear sound and three-word example pattern. The app itself was not installed or tested.

## S07 — BBC pronunciation collection

[BBC Learning English: Pronunciation](https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/learningenglish/english/features/pronunciation/). Official BBC-hosted page opened; includes older and newer episode listings. Supports the sound-video lesson model and the distinction between individual sounds and connected speech. No video redistribution rights established.

## S08 — Cambridge pronunciation portal

[Cambridge Dictionary pronunciation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/pronunciation/). Search-indexed publisher description; direct opening returned 403. Supports real-speaker UK/US word pronunciation and dictionary transcription references.

## S09 — Cambridge symbol key

[Cambridge phonetics help](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/help/phonetics.html). Search-indexed text; direct opening returned 403. Supports IPA-based dictionary notation. Reopen during content review if exact examples or variants are needed.

## S10 — Cambridge sheep entry

[Sheep pronunciation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/sheep). Indexed word-level UK `/ʃiːp/` and sound segmentation; direct opening returned 403. Textual cross-check only; no audio audition.

## S11 — Oxford pronunciation conventions

[Oxford pronunciation guide](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/about/pronunciation_english.html). Opened. Supports broad dictionary notation, optional linking /r/, weak vowels, syllabic consonants, and variants affecting the traditional diphthong chart. It is a reference, not a copied teaching dataset.

## S12 — Oxford vocabulary selection

[About the Oxford 3000 and 5000](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/about/wordlists/oxford3000-5000). Opened. Oxford 3000 is a frequency/relevance-selected A1–B2 list. Use its levels to check individual senses, not to label every suggested word A1 or to import the entire list.

## S13 — Cambridge data reuse

[Cambridge Dictionary API FAQ](https://dictionary-api.cambridge.org/api/faq). Search-indexed publisher FAQ; direct open failed. The indexed FAQ describes negotiated offline-storage licensing. It establishes no permission for this project. Obtain and review applicable current terms if a licensed integration is pursued.

## S14 — ipachart.com

[Interactive IPA Chart](https://www.ipachart.com/). Opened. Full international sound-chart reference, with an aspiration caveat and per-clip Commons attribution guidance. Source for competitor observations, not a blanket licence for its media collection.

## S15 — EnglishClub

[Interactive Phonemic Chart](https://www.englishclub.com/pronunciation/phonemic-chart-ia.php). Opened outer page; embedded chart was not exercised. British RP chart description and sound/example-word interaction are documented by the publisher. No offline reuse permission established.

## S16 — phonemicchart.com

[Phonemic Chart Keyboard](https://www.phonemicchart.com/). Opened. British/American vowel controls, hints, symbol entry, and copy features are visible in retrieved page structure. No live clipboard or audio test performed.

## S17 — toPhonetics

[English text to IPA converter](https://tophonetics.com/). Opened. British/American options, weak forms, parallel text, alternate pronunciations, and browser speech synthesis are described. Conversion output and speech were not tested. Do not equate speech synthesis with recorded dictionary audio.

## S18 — Articulation reference

[University of Iowa: Sounds of Speech](https://soundsofspeech.uiowa.edu/index.php). Indexed university description; opened page returned no readable body. American-English articulation animations/video/audio are described; English is presented as a mobile offering. Secondary articulation reference, not the project's British audio source.

## S19 — Learning science

[John Dunlosky: Strengthening the Student Toolbox](https://www.aft.org/ae/fall2013/dunlosky). Opened. Author's account of practice testing and distributed practice, based on the 2013 review. Supports retrieval and spacing broadly, not a particular IPA lesson length, scheduling algorithm, or mastery percentage.

## S20 — Accessibility standard

[W3C: WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/). Opened. Basis for keyboard operation, focus, text alternatives, contrast, reflow, and target-size requirements. Meeting a few listed criteria alone is not a conformance audit.

## S21 — IPA font

[SIL Charis FAQ](https://software.sil.org/charis/faq/) and [official source repository](https://github.com/silnrsi/font-charis). FAQ opened; official repository search-indexed. Candidate font under OFL 1.1. Retain the exact release's licence and copyright notices if later bundled; test IPA glyph coverage in the actual release.

## S22 — Reusable phrase sample: a ship

[Commons: En-uk-a ship.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:En-uk-a_ship.ogg). Opened file description. Credited to Association Shtooka, Judith Franck; London speaker; CC BY 3.0 US. It contains a phrase, not a bare word or isolated vowel. Candidate metadata checked, listening review pending.

## S23 — Reusable phrase sample: a sheep

[Commons: En-uk-a sheep.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:En-uk-a_sheep.ogg). Opened file description. Same credited speaker/collection and CC BY 3.0 US. Candidate metadata checked, listening review pending.

## S24 — Reusable generic [i]

[Commons: Close front unrounded vowel.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Close_front_unrounded_vowel.ogg). Opened file description. Recording credited to Denelson83; a CC BY-SA 3.0 option is offered alongside GFDL. A phonetic reference, not independently validated British `/iː/` teaching audio.

## S25 — Reusable generic [ɪ]

[Commons: Near-close near-front unrounded vowel.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Near-close_near-front_unrounded_vowel.ogg). Opened file description. Denelson83; CC BY-SA 3.0 option. Same distinction between a generic phone and an accent-specific phoneme target applies.

## S26 — Local application tooling

[Vite getting started](https://vite.dev/guide/). Opened. Official source for local development, vanilla TypeScript templates, static builds, and runtime requirements. Select and lock actual dependency versions during implementation rather than treating this research as a package lock.

## S27 — Audio playback behavior

[MDN: HTMLMediaElement preservesPitch](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/HTMLMediaElement/preservesPitch). Opened. Supports pitch-preserving playback-rate controls. Actual clarity and browser behavior still require testing with the chosen recordings.
