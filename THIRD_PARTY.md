## Device word speech and QR

Additional example words can be read by the user's browser/OS `SpeechSynthesis` service with an explicitly available British English voice. These are synthesized readings, labelled separately from the 37 human recordings above. Voices are supplied by the device/browser, are not bundled or redistributed, and can require an installed language pack or network access. The app submits only the selected existing example word; it does not record the learner.

`public/website-qr.svg` and `public/website-qr.png` encode the public website address. They were generated with ReportLab's QR implementation and Pillow (authoring tools, neither included as a website dependency). The authoring script is `scripts/generate-qr.py`; these assets contain no third-party visual artwork.

## Frontal mouth photographs — UCL MaFI

44 JPEG stills in `public/mouth-photos/` derive from selected British English B1 word recordings in *Mouth and Facial Informativeness: Norming Study* by **Anna Krason, Ye Zhang, Hillarie Man and Gabriella Vigliocco**, UCL Language & Cognition Lab. [OSF repository](https://osf.io/mna8j/), [publisher paper](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-023-02216-z), [open paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11289175/). © The authors, 2023, **[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)**. No endorsement is implied.

The [repository metadata](https://api.osf.io/v2/nodes/mna8j/) explicitly includes video stimuli and still frames in CC BY 4.0 and permits teaching-material reproduction with attribution; its linked [licence record](https://api.osf.io/v2/licenses/563c1cf88c5e4a3877f9e96a/) confirms the licence. The paper identifies a native British actress for B1–B3 and records consent to publish photographs. American A1/A2 material is not used. This is permission for the source media, distinct from the standard YouTube embed permission above.

Changes: full-size 1280×720 or 1920×1080 JPEG frames extracted from the licensed MP4s with FFmpeg (`-ss TIME -frames:v 1 -q:v 2`); no face/mouth retouching, generation or colour changes. CSS magnifies a mouth-region viewport equivalent to 600×400 at (660,640) on a 1920×1080 reference frame (scaled proportionally for 720p sources), while each intact full-frame JPEG remains linked. Word, exact time/frame, source member/CRC, acquired source-video SHA-256 and shipped-image SHA-256 are recorded in `content/mouth-photos.json`. Only chosen ZIP members were retrieved through ordinary public HTTP byte ranges and checked against the ZIP directory CRC; the complete 2.56 GB archive was not downloaded or claimed as hash-verified. The original video files remain ignored working material.
